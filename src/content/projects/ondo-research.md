## Context

At the start of a consulting engagement, a lot of the research is the same every time:
learning the client's company, its market, and its competitors before anyone can give
useful product advice. Ondo Research is the platform we built to do that groundwork
automatically, so the team can spend its time on the actual recommendations.

## The Product

A new engagement starts with a single intake form. You describe the client once:
company details, industry, consumer market, product direction, constraints, known
competitors, and the goals of the engagement, plus any documents, slides, spreadsheets,
or images they've shared.

Submitting the form creates the project and immediately starts the company and market
scrapers, with the known competitors seeding the market search. A chatbot is available
right away and gets better as findings come in, so questions about the client and their
market are answered from the evidence the platform has gathered rather than from
guesswork. Projects, proposed solutions, and chat history are organized in one
workspace that the whole organization can access.
